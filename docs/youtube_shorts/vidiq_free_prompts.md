# vidIQ Free Prompt Bank

Use these prompts against individual Shorts, not broad channel audits. Free vidIQ credits are limited, so spend them on the strongest candidates.

## Hook and Clarity

`Does the first second clearly explain what the viewer is seeing? Be specific about what is confusing to a cold YouTube Shorts viewer.`

`I run a YouTube Shorts channel where every dot is a real follower and only one survives. Based on this Short idea, rewrite the first 2 seconds so the format is instantly understandable.`

## Titles

`Give me 5 YouTube Shorts title options for this video. Do not use Day numbers or series language. Focus on action + stakes + curiosity.`

`Which of these 3 titles is strongest for Shorts and why: [paste titles]?`

## Retention

`Where is the likely retention drop in this Short, and how would you rewrite the opening 3 seconds to reduce swipes?`

`What payoff is missing from this Short? Suggest one stronger ending line or reveal.`

## Competitive Framing

`What would make this Short feel native to YouTube instead of a repost from Instagram? Give 3 concrete changes.`

`If this Short underperforms, is the bigger problem likely hook clarity, stakes, pacing, or payoff? Rank those from most likely to least likely and explain briefly.`
